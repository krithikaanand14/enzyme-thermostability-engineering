# Enzyme Thermostability Engineering

**Master's thesis portfolio | Krithika Anand | Chemical Biotechnology**

A high-level overview of experience in rational enzyme engineering and biochemical characterization. This repository presents the scientific approach and skills developed without disclosing unpublished designs, experimental results, or internal research details.

## Research question

How can enzyme thermal stability be investigated alongside functional performance during protein engineering?

## Project overview

The thesis investigated a terpene synthase using a workflow that connected computationally guided candidate selection, molecular biology, recombinant expression, protein purification, and analytical characterization. Thermal behavior and functional output were evaluated as separate aspects of enzyme performance.

## My contribution

- Generated candidate enzyme variants through site-directed mutagenesis and molecular cloning.
- Carried out recombinant expression and screening in E. coli.
- Performed protein purification and assessed sample quality.
- Used differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) to characterize thermal stability.
- Used gas chromatography–mass spectrometry (GC–MS) to assess product formation and profiles.
- Organized experimental measurements and interpreted stability and functional readouts together.

## Workflow at a glance

Candidate selection → molecular cloning → recombinant expression → protein purification → thermal and functional characterization → comparative interpretation

This is a conceptual overview, not an experimental protocol.

## Skills demonstrated

Site-directed mutagenesis · Molecular cloning · Recombinant protein expression · Chromatographic purification · DSC · GC–MS · Experimental troubleshooting · Scientific interpretation

## Scientific perspective

Thermal stability and functional performance answer different questions. A useful engineering assessment examines both rather than treating a thermal measurement alone as evidence of improved enzyme performance. This overview makes no claims about specific variants, measured improvements, or project outcomes.
